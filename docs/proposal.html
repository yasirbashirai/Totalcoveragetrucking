03 · The benchmark
Website evaluation · knighttrans.com
What Knight gets right,
and where you will beat them

Knight Transportation runs one of the largest truckload fleets in North America, over 3,100 tractors and 24 terminals. I reviewed their website in detail. Here is what is worth copying, and what is worth doing better.

Worth copying
  • Two front doors. Clear separate paths for drivers ("Drive With Knight") and shippers ("Ship With Knight").
  • Application near the top. The driver application sits high on the homepage, not buried in a menu.
  • Numbers as proof. Fleet size, terminal count and equipment age build instant trust.
  • Clean, modern look. White space, real photography, simple icons, card layouts.
Where it falls short
  • No human proof. No driver quotes, no customer testimonials, only statistics.
  • Application friction. Applying sends drivers to an external system with extra steps.
  • No quick answers. No FAQ and no fast contact route for a driver with one simple question.
  • Built for a giant. Generic national messaging, nothing local, nothing personal.

How Total Coverage Trucking will excel

Knight TransportationYour site will do better
National and generic, speaks to everyoneLocal and specific: same day, anywhere in Florida, on every page
Driver application on an external systemDriver application built into your own site, phone friendly, done in minutes
Stats only, no faces or voicesA personal startup story: real people, real trucks, testimonials as you grow
No FAQ, slow paths to answersDriver FAQ plus a tap to call button on every page
Corporate distance, layers between peopleDirect access: drivers and carriers reach the owner, and the site says so

In short: we borrow Knight's structure and polish, then win on speed, locality and the personal touch that a national fleet cannot offer.
